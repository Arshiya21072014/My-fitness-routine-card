What is fitness:

Fitness is the ability of your body's systems to work together efficiently, allowing you to perform daily activities with vigor, alertness, and minimal fatigue.

What is fitness routine:

A fitness routine is a regular, structured plan of physical exercise and movement designed to improve health, build strength, increase endurance, and maintain overall physical well-being.

Reason to be fit:

Getting in shape improves your physical health, boosts mental well-being, and extends your lifespan according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Top Reasons to Stay Fit: • Better Heart Health: Regular physical activity lowers your risk of heart disease, stroke, and high blood pressure. • Improved Mood: Exercise reduces feelings of anxiety, helps you sleep better, and lowers long-term risks of depression. • Stronger Bones and Muscles: Weight-bearing and strength exercises slow bone density loss and ease joint pain. • Sharp Brain Function: Staying active keeps learning and memory skills sharp as you age. • Longer Life: Moving more each day cuts the risk of early death from major chronic illnesses.
